Implemented with a `LEFT JOIN LATERAL … ORDER BY currency NULLS LAST LIMIT 1` so a currency-specific mapping always wins over an all-currencies one when both exist, keeping resolution deterministic (Module Inv. #10) even though seeding (ac03) is what actually decides whether that overlap ever occurs in practice.
