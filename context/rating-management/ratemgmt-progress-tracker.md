## Architecture Decisions

- **2026-08-27 — rm04's infra lives in `enterprise-billing-app`, not a separate "rating repo."** The spec (`rm04-spec` header table, D1) and `ratemgmt-code-standards.md` §8/§1.2 both frame `worker/**`/`infra/**`/`dev/**` as a separate rating-repo boundary, kept apart from the app repo because rating *flow logic* must never live here (`ratemgmt-ai-workflow-rules.md` §1.2).
